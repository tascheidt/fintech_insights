# Fintech Taxonomy Category Definitions

**Date:** January 27, 2026  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive definitions for all 45 fintech-specific job categories

---

## Group 1: Engineering (10 categories)

### `engineering-backend`
**Definition:** Backend engineering roles focused on server-side development, APIs, and data processing.  
**Technologies:** Java, Go, Node.js, Python, Ruby, PHP, APIs, microservices  
**Examples:** Backend Engineer, API Engineer, Server Engineer

### `engineering-frontend`
**Definition:** Frontend engineering roles focused on user-facing web applications.  
**Technologies:** React, Vue, Angular, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML/CSS  
**Examples:** Frontend Engineer, UI Engineer, Web Developer

### `engineering-fullstack`
**Definition:** Full-stack engineering roles working across both frontend and backend.  
**Technologies:** Full stack technologies (React + Node, etc.)  
**Examples:** Full Stack Engineer, Software Engineer (generalist)

### `engineering-mobile`
**Definition:** Mobile application development for iOS, Android, or cross-platform.  
**Technologies:** Swift, Kotlin, React Native, Flutter, iOS, Android  
**Examples:** Mobile Engineer, iOS Engineer, Android Engineer

### `engineering-data`
**Definition:** Data engineering roles focused on pipelines, ETL, and data warehousing.  
**Technologies:** SQL, Spark, Airflow, dbt, data pipelines, ETL  
**Examples:** Data Engineer, ETL Engineer, Data Pipeline Engineer  
**Edge Cases:** SQL-focused roles → this category (NOT data-analytics-bi)

### `engineering-ai-ml`
**Definition:** AI/ML engineering roles building models, algorithms, and ML systems.  
**Technologies:** Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, LLMs, ML models, algorithms  
**Examples:** ML Engineer, AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer  
**Distinction:** Building ML systems (engineering-ai-ml) vs using ML for business (data-science)

### `engineering-platform-sre-devops`
**Definition:** Platform engineering, SRE, and DevOps roles focused on infrastructure, reliability, and cloud.  
**Technologies:** Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, GCP, Terraform, infrastructure, monitoring  
**Examples:** Platform Engineer, SRE, DevOps Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer  
**Distinction:** Infrastructure/reliability focus (this) vs application development (engineering-backend)

### `engineering-security`
**Definition:** Security engineering roles focused on application security, InfoSec, and cryptography.  
**Technologies:** Security tools, cryptography, AppSec, InfoSec  
**Examples:** Security Engineer, AppSec Engineer, InfoSec Engineer

### `engineering-qa`
**Definition:** Quality assurance and test engineering roles.  
**Technologies:** Testing frameworks, automation, SDET tools  
**Examples:** QA Engineer, Test Engineer, SDET

### `engineering-management`
**Definition:** Engineering management roles (EMs, Directors, VPs of Engineering).  
**Examples:** Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering, VP Engineering

## Group 2: Product & Design (5 categories)

### `product-management`
**Definition:** Product management roles defining product strategy and roadmap.  
**Examples:** Product Manager, Product Owner, Senior PM  
**Edge Cases:** Product Engineer (if strategic) → this category

### `product-design-ux`
**Definition:** Product design and UX roles focused on user experience and interface design.  
**Examples:** Product Designer, UX Designer, UI Designer

### `product-research`
**Definition:** User research roles focused on understanding user needs and behaviors.  
**Examples:** User Researcher, UX Researcher

### `technical-program-management`
**Definition:** Technical program management roles coordinating complex technical initiatives (e.g., bank migrations, integrations).  
**Examples:** Technical Program Manager, TPM, Program Manager (technical)

### `technical-writing`
**Definition:** Technical writing roles creating documentation, especially for developer platforms.  
**Examples:** Technical Writer, API Documentation Writer

---

## Group 3: Data & Analytics (3 categories)

### `data-science`
**Definition:** Data science roles focused on predictive modeling, ML for business (e.g., credit scoring, risk models).  
**Technologies:** Python, R, ML models, predictive analytics  
**Examples:** Data Scientist, ML Scientist, Risk Modeler  
**Distinction:** Predictive modeling for business (this) vs building ML systems (engineering-ai-ml)

### `data-analytics-bi`
**Definition:** Data analytics and business intelligence roles focused on reporting, dashboards, and analysis.  
**Technologies:** Looker, Tableau, SQL, Excel, BI tools  
**Examples:** Data Analyst, BI Analyst, Business Analyst  
**Edge Cases:** Excel/Financial Modeling → finance-accounting (NOT this)

### `analytics-engineering`
**Definition:** Analytics engineering roles focused on data modeling and transformation (e.g., dbt).  
**Technologies:** dbt, data modeling, transformation  
**Examples:** Analytics Engineer, Data Modeler

---

## Group 4: Risk, Legal & Compliance (6 categories)

### `compliance`
**Definition:** Compliance roles focused on regulatory compliance, KYC, KYB operations.  
**Examples:** Compliance Officer, KYC Analyst, Compliance Operations  
**Distinction:** Compliance operations (this) vs legal counsel (legal)

### `aml-financial-crime`
**Definition:** Anti-Money Laundering and financial crime prevention roles.  
**Examples:** AML Analyst, Financial Crime Analyst, Sanctions Analyst

### `fraud-trust-safety`
**Definition:** Fraud detection and trust & safety operations.  
**Examples:** Fraud Analyst, Trust & Safety Specialist, Fraud Operations  
**Distinction:** Fraud operations (this) vs risk management (risk-management)

### `legal`
**Definition:** Legal counsel roles focused on corporate law, contracts, and litigation.  
**Examples:** Legal Counsel, Corporate Counsel, Contracts Manager  
**Distinction:** Legal counsel (this) vs compliance operations (compliance)

### `regulatory-affairs`
**Definition:** Regulatory affairs roles focused on government relations and licensing.  
**Examples:** Regulatory Affairs Manager, Government Relations, Licensing Specialist

### `risk-management`
**Definition:** Risk management roles focused on credit risk, market risk, and liquidity risk.  
**Examples:** Risk Manager, Credit Risk Analyst, Market Risk Analyst  
**Distinction:** Risk management (this) vs fraud operations (fraud-trust-safety) vs risk modeling (data-science)

---

## Group 5: Go-To-Market (8 categories)

### `sales-account-executives`
**Definition:** Sales roles focused on new business acquisition.  
**Examples:** Account Executive, Sales Representative, Business Development (sales-focused)

### `account-management-customer-success`
**Definition:** Account management and customer success roles focused on retention and upsell.  
**Examples:** Customer Success Manager, Account Manager, CSM  
**Distinction:** Retention/upsell (this) vs support tickets (customer-support-cx)

### `customer-support`
**Definition:** Customer support roles handling tickets and customer inquiries.  
**Examples:** Customer Support Specialist, Support Engineer  
**Note:** Also exists as `customer-support-cx` in Operations & People group - use this for Go-To-Market context

### `business-development-partnerships`
**Definition:** Business development and partnerships roles (e.g., bank alliances, network partners).  
**Examples:** Business Development Manager, Partnerships Manager, Strategic Partnerships  
**Distinction:** Partnerships/alliances (this) vs sales (sales-account-executives)

### `solutions-engineering`
**Definition:** Solutions engineering roles providing pre-sales technical support.  
**Examples:** Solutions Engineer, Sales Engineer, Pre-Sales Engineer

### `revenue-operations`
**Definition:** Revenue operations roles managing sales tools and processes (RevOps).  
**Examples:** Revenue Operations Manager, RevOps Specialist, Sales Operations

### `marketing-growth-performance`
**Definition:** Marketing roles focused on growth, performance marketing, and paid acquisition.  
**Examples:** Growth Marketer, Performance Marketing Manager, Paid Acquisition Specialist

### `marketing-product-brand`
**Definition:** Marketing roles focused on product marketing, content, brand, and communications.  
**Examples:** Product Marketing Manager, Content Marketer, Brand Manager, PR Manager

### `developer-relations`
**Definition:** Developer relations roles for API-first fintechs (e.g., Stripe, Plaid).  
**Examples:** Developer Relations Engineer, DevRel, Developer Advocate, Community Manager (technical)

---

## Group 6: Finance & Strategy (5 categories)

### `finance-accounting`
**Definition:** Finance and accounting roles focused on accounting, tax, and financial operations.  
**Technologies:** Excel, financial modeling, accounting systems  
**Examples:** Controller, Accountant, Tax Specialist, AP/AR Specialist  
**Edge Cases:** Excel/Financial Modeling → this category (NOT data-analytics-bi)

### `strategic-finance-fpa`
**Definition:** Strategic finance and FP&A roles focused on planning, forecasting, and modeling.  
**Examples:** FP&A Analyst, Strategic Finance Manager, Financial Planning Analyst

### `capital-markets-treasury`
**Definition:** Capital markets and treasury roles managing debt facilities and liquidity (crucial for lending/neobanks).  
**Examples:** Capital Markets Manager, Treasury Manager, Liquidity Manager

### `corporate-development-strategy`
**Definition:** Corporate development and strategy roles focused on M&A and long-term strategy.  
**Examples:** Corporate Development Manager, Strategy Manager, M&A Analyst

### `investor-relations`
**Definition:** Investor relations roles managing relationships with investors.  
**Examples:** Investor Relations Manager, IR Specialist

---

## Group 7: Operations & People (8 categories)

### `customer-support-cx`
**Definition:** Customer support and customer experience roles (tickets, call center).  
**Examples:** Customer Support Specialist, CX Specialist, Call Center Representative  
**Distinction:** Support operations (this) vs customer success (account-management-customer-success)

### `customer-operations`
**Definition:** Customer operations roles handling manual reviews (e.g., KYC review, dispute resolution).  
**Examples:** Customer Operations Specialist, KYC Reviewer, Dispute Resolution Specialist

### `people-ops-hr`
**Definition:** People operations and HR roles.  
**Examples:** HR Manager, People Ops Specialist, HR Business Partner

### `talent-acquisition-recruiting`
**Definition:** Talent acquisition and recruiting roles.  
**Examples:** Recruiter, Talent Acquisition Specialist, Recruiting Coordinator

### `it-internal-systems`
**Definition:** IT and internal systems roles (helpdesk, corporate IT).  
**Examples:** IT Specialist, Helpdesk Support, Internal Systems Administrator

### `business-operations`
**Definition:** Business operations roles (BizOps, generalist operations).  
**Examples:** Business Operations Manager, BizOps Specialist, Operations Manager

### `executive-leadership`
**Definition:** Executive and leadership roles (C-Suite, Founders).  
**Examples:** CEO, CTO, CFO, VP, Director (executive level)

### `administrative`
**Definition:** Administrative roles (EA, Office Manager).  
**Examples:** Executive Assistant, Office Manager, Administrative Assistant

---

## Other

### `other`
**Definition:** Roles that cannot be categorized into any of the above 44 categories.  
**Use:** Only when truly cannot be categorized. Prefer specific categories when possible.

---

## Edge Case Rules Summary

1. **SQL/Data Pipelines** → `engineering-data` (NOT `data-analytics-bi`)
2. **Excel/Financial Modeling** → `finance-accounting` (NOT `data-analytics-bi`)
3. **Product Engineer** → `engineering-fullstack` (if technical) OR `product-management` (if strategic)
4. **Marketing Engineer** → `engineering-frontend` or `engineering-fullstack` (NOT marketing)
5. **Platform Engineering/SRE** → `engineering-platform-sre-devops` (distinct from generic DevOps)
6. **Risk Modeling** → `data-science` (NOT `risk-management`)
7. **Fraud Detection** → `fraud-trust-safety` (NOT `risk-management`)
8. **Compliance Operations** → `compliance` (NOT `legal`)
9. **Legal Counsel** → `legal` (NOT `compliance`)
10. **Customer Support (tickets)** → `customer-support-cx` (NOT `account-management-customer-success`)
11. **Customer Success (retention)** → `account-management-customer-success` (NOT `customer-support-cx`)
